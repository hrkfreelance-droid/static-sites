04Interior Paint

Match the physical 3435 C chip.

The physical PANTONE 3435 C chip is the master. 塗料は現物チップの発色に合わせて選定・調合します。Use the physical PANTONE 3435 C chip as the master reference. Select or custom-mix paint to match it.

  1. Physical chip

    画面や印刷した見本ではなく、PANTONE 3435 C の現物チップを使用。Use the physical PANTONE 3435 C chip, not a screen or printed screenshot.

  2. Match in daylight

    自然光で色合わせ。既製色または調色で 3435 C に合わせる。Match in daylight. Use ready-mixed or custom-tinted paint to reach the 3435 C appearance.

  3. Test after drying

    試し塗りし、完全乾燥後に現物チップと比較。Apply a test area and compare with the physical chip after the paint is fully dry.

  4. Matte finish only.

    仕上げはつや消しのみ。光沢・半光沢は使用しない。Use a matte finish only. Do not use gloss or semi-gloss finishes.
